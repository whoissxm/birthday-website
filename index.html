Dear Tanishka,

Happy Birthday to someone who has made this past year so much more special than I could have ever imagined. Today is your day, and I just wanted to take a little time to tell you how much you mean to me.

When I thought about what I could give you for your birthday, I didn't want to make it just another message that you read and forget. I wanted to make something that you could look back at someday and remember all the little moments, laughs, conversations and memories that made this year special.

One year might not sound like a very long time, but when I think about everything that has happened during it, there are so many memories packed into that one year. Some moments were big, some were completely random, and some were just ordinary little moments that somehow became memorable.

And that's probably one of my favourite things about our memories. They don't have to be perfect to be special. Sometimes it's the smallest things that end up being the moments we remember the most.

Every picture on this website is a little reminder of those moments. Looking at them makes me realise how quickly time passes and how many memories can be created without even noticing it at the time.

Thank you for being such an important part of my life. Thank you for all the laughs, the conversations, the silly moments, and even the completely random things that somehow became memories worth keeping.

I hope you know how genuinely special you are. You deserve a birthday filled with happiness, laughter, good memories and people who make you feel appreciated and loved.

I hope this new year of your life brings you everything you've been wishing for. I hope there are many more reasons for you to smile, many more places to see, things to experience, and countless moments that you'll look back on with a smile.

And most importantly, I hope you always stay the same amazing person you are. Never forget how special you are to the people around you.

So, on your birthday, I just want to say thank you for being you and for becoming such a memorable part of my life. This little website may not be anything huge, but I made every part of it with you in mind.

Here's to the memories we've already made, the moments we're going to remember years from now, and all the beautiful memories still waiting ahead.

Happy Birthday once again ❤️

With lots of love,
— Sam
